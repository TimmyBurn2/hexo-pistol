## The question

Is `random_openings_v3.txt` a **fixture** under hard rule 7 — committed,
digest-pinned, regenerated byte for byte by a test — or a **book** under hard
rule 8 — uncommitted under `artifacts/`, sha-indexed by a committed manifest that
`BookVersion::V3` resolves through?

## §R — what revision 1 got wrong

Recorded before the corrected argument, because a matrix that quietly repairs
itself is the defect D-634 and D-635 both name.

**R1. The central argument was a misreading of D-151.** Revision 1 answered the
"the filter consults an engine" attack by arguing that v3 *"would never be
regenerated when the engine changes"*. D-151's flip clause is **unconditional**
— *"flips the moment a curation step consults an engine"* — and
"regenerated whenever the engine changes" belongs to a conjunctive DESCRIPTION of
what a book is, not to the flip's trigger. Revision 1 promoted a
sufficient-for-bookhood property into a necessary condition on the flip. The
argument is withdrawn. The correct answer is §D's measurement, which removes the
engine-derived input rather than excusing it.

**R2. F8 measured the wrong quantity.** Revision 1 reported EXACT-LINE overlap
(0 against v1, 2 against v2) and concluded the filter was *"close to a null
operation"*. D-644 mandates disjointness under **canonical form**. Revision 1's
own F9 recorded that exact-line understates canonical by 10× on the only pair
anyone had measured, and it reported the exact-line figure anyway.

**MEASURED, canonical, verified twice** (red team, then this session):

| pair | canonical overlap |
|---|---|
| v1 ∩ v2 | **10** — reproduces the committed pin, which is what validates the instrument |
| v1 ∩ candidate v3 | **9** |
| v2 ∩ candidate v3 | **29** |
| corpus₅ ∩ candidate v3 | **23** |

So the filter must reject **38** openings, not 2. A filter is therefore
MANDATORY, and re-rolling the seed to reach zero is arithmetically closed:
λ = 8500 · 6490 / 1 277 595 ≈ 43 expected collisions, so P(0) ≈ e⁻⁴³ (DERIVED).

**R3. "The digest refusal already exists three times over" conflated a refusal
with two tests.** F3 is a run-time refusal (`ArenaError::OpeningsDigest`). F4 and
F5 are GATES: they fail CI, they refuse nothing at run time. The honest count is
**one refusal and two gates**. D-645 asks for a refusal *"with a named error"*,
and only F3 answers it.

**R4. Two ungraded numbers.** Revision 1's "570 KB" for three books is
**545 634 B = 532.8 KiB** MEASURED, and it carried no grade in a document that
grades everything (D-291).

**R5. A false citation.** `book_v3_registration.md` cited gate 5's green as
evidence for the fixture class. Gate 5 carries content signatures for
`arena_report` and `baseline_snapshot` and **for nothing else**; it cannot
recognise an opening book, so its green is equally compatible with the books
being rule-8 artefacts wrongly committed. That is D-203's recorded history
re-run, and the citation is struck.

**R6. D-518 was never cited, and it is the most on-point precedent against the
selected option.** See §C.

## §D — THE MEASUREMENT THAT DECIDES IT

A game passes through exactly one 5-stone position: its own opening, which the
book chose and the engine did not. The corpus was labelled from `book_v2`
openings `13..3499`. So its 5-stone slice should BE those openings, and it is —
MEASURED over all 89 805 deduped rows, bucketed by token count in the `key_full`
column:

```
corpus 5-stone distinct canonical keys : 3487
corpus₅ ∩ book_v2                      : 3487
corpus₅ \ book_v2                      :    0     ← nothing outside
(corpus₅ ∩ v3) \ (book_v2 ∩ v3)        :    0     ← the decisive one
```

**D-644's corpus clause is extensionally empty given D-644's own `book_v2`
clause.** The engine-derived criterion cannot remove one opening that the
non-engine-derived criterion does not already remove.

This is what answers D-151's flip — by DELETION rather than by revision 1's
misreading. There is no engine-derived input to the generator, so nothing for the
flip to fire on. Its scope is exactly D-175's: the containment holds because
`k_stones = 5`, and would stop holding if k moved or a future corpus were
labelled from a different opening set.

### Option D — SELECTED. Fixture, filtered against the two COMMITTED books only; the corpus clause satisfied by PROOF rather than by filtering

- **Generator filter inputs**: `random_openings_v1.txt` and
  `random_openings_v2.txt` — both committed, both digest-pinned, both present in
  any clone. Over-generate, reject a candidate whose canonical form is in either,
  and REFUSE any survivor count that is not 8500 rather than truncating — so
  `n_openings` is solved for the draw at which exactly 8500 survive, and the gap
  between the two numbers is the rejection count and nothing else. (Revision 2 of
  this matrix said "truncate to 8500". The implementation took the safer reading
  and this line follows the CODE, per REVIEW-impl M-2: a prefix of the survivors
  would be a sample size nobody chose.)
- **The corpus clause is DISCHARGED, not struck.** An independent `tools/` script
  reports all four counts including the corpus one, and its receipt records
  `v3 vs corpus: 0 of 8500`. It is **not** a CI gate, because it needs the 43 MB
  out-of-tree corpus. D-644 is satisfied as written; nothing is weakened.
- **Cost**, corrected against v2's own landing commit `3df39c8`
  (15 files, +5525/−48, MEASURED): three match arms, one config, one fixture, the
  regeneration test and digest constant, **a `docs/book_v3_ledger.md`** (revision
  1 omitted this; v1 had no ledger and D-491/D-492 had to reconstruct its ranges
  after the fact), and **the filter itself, which is new surface with no sibling
  to copy** — the criticism revision 1 levelled only at B.
- **Gate deltas, MEASURED**: gate 6 needs no edit (`config_check.sh:61` globs
  `random_openings_*.toml`); gate 17 needs no rule-9 entry (it reads `*.rs`/`*.sh`
  only); gate 16 pins v1 by name and is untouched; gate 5 passes a third
  committed book; gate 3 grows by **~1.5 s**.

## §C — THE DEPARTURE FROM D-518, ADMITTED

D-518 considered this exact filter for `book_v2` and **declined it**:

> A filter against v1 was DECLINED because it would make this fixture's bytes
> depend on another file's, which is the property D-147/D-152/D-177 exist to
> keep; re-rolling the seed until the overlap is zero was declined because §5
> registered that the seed is recorded rather than chosen for any property of the
> book it makes.

Option D departs from that, knowingly, because D-644 is later, is specific to
`book_v3`, and rules the other way. **Two costs are paid and neither is hidden.**

1. **v3 cannot carry the sentence both committed books carry in their own
   bytes** — `random_openings_v{1,2}.txt:6`, emitted from `document.rs`: *"book
   has no external input, so 'the committed bytes are the bytes this build
   writes' is a test rather than an errand."* v3's preamble must state its two
   inputs instead. The property degrades from "reproducible from its config" to
   **"reproducible from its config and two committed, digest-pinned files in this
   same repository"** — weaker, still fully checkable on a fresh clone, and the
   reason C-1 does not bite Option D.
2. **The seed is no longer innocent.** Revision 1 measured seed `20260906`'s
   overlap properties before any registration selected it, against D-518 §5's
   *"recorded rather than chosen"*. This is defused by the filter rather than
   argued away: **with a mandatory filter the overlap is 0 by construction for
   every seed**, so no seed can be chosen for that property and the measurement
   confers no advantage. The seed remains the authoring date, by v1's and v2's
   convention.

## Selection

**Option D.** It is the only option that yields a `book_v3` a fresh clone can
regenerate and verify, because its filter reads only committed files.
